About Heavenly Cleaners

A Nairobi-based cleaning company built on trust, care, and attention to detail.

Heavenly Cleaners Co. was started by a group of friends who believed that a clean space is more than just tidy — it's a place where you can breathe, rest, and feel at peace.

From homes to offices, from post-construction dust to delicate upholstery, we bring the same promise to every job: reliable service, trusted hands, and professional results.

We're based in Nairobi, Kenya, and we serve both residential and commercial clients across the city and surrounding areas.
